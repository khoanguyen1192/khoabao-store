Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026
Last updated: August 21, 2026

Balance & Chill is designed to be playable without creating an account. We do not sell personal information, serve third-party advertising, or track you across apps and websites.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Balance & Chill (“the App”), developed by Khoa Nguyen (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the App on iPhone or iPad. It applies only to Balance & Chill and not to third-party services governed by their own policies.

2. Information we collect

We do not operate a user-account system or our own backend for the App, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

Information stored locally

The App stores limited gameplay preferences and progress on your device, including:

  • Your personal best score
  • Gameplay preferences, such as whether motion controls are enabled

This information is stored locally using Apple's app-specific storage and is not transmitted to us. It may be removed if you delete the App, clear app data, or reset your device.

Device motion

If you enable tilt controls, the App processes device-motion readings to control the balance beam. Motion readings are used in real time on your device. We do not store them or transmit them to our servers.

3. Game Center

The App offers optional Apple Game Center features for player authentication and a global leaderboard. If you use Game Center, the App may submit your score and interact with the Game Center player identity provided by Apple. Apple processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your Apple ID password or operate a separate copy of your Game Center account.

You can play the core game without using Game Center. Game Center availability and account controls are managed through your Apple device settings.

4. Firebase

The App includes Firebase Core to initialize the app's Firebase configuration. Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Firebase advertising features are not enabled in the current version. We do not use Firebase to build advertising profiles or collect gameplay analytics.

5. Advertising and tracking

The current version of Balance & Chill does not include third-party advertisements and does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If advertising or analytics features are introduced in a future version, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before those features are released.

6. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not operate accounts or directly collect personal information, we do not use children's data for marketing or profiling. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the method below.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is overwritten, the App is deleted, or the device data is erased. We do not maintain a server-side database of this local data, so we cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

Game Center data is controlled by Apple. Please use your Apple account and Game Center settings, or contact Apple, for requests concerning Game Center data.

8. Security

We minimize data handling by keeping gameplay preferences and personal-best progress on the device. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure, but we limit the information processed to what is necessary to provide the App's features.

9. International users

The App may be available in multiple countries and regions. Third-party platform services, including Apple Game Center, may process information in accordance with their own global infrastructure and privacy terms.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date and last-updated date at the top of this page will identify the latest version. Material changes will be reflected before the applicable App update is released.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests relating to Balance & Chill, open a privacy support request.
