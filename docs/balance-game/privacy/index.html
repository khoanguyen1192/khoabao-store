Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026
Last updated: September 2, 2026

Balance & Chill is playable without creating an account. We do not sell personal information. The App may display third-party advertising and offers an optional one-time purchase to remove ads.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Balance & Chill (“the App”), developed by Khoa Nguyen (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the App on iPhone or iPad. It applies only to Balance & Chill and not to third-party services governed by their own policies.

2. Information we collect

We do not operate a user-account system or our own backend for the App, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

Information stored locally

The App stores limited gameplay preferences and progress on your device, including:

  • Scores, Daily Challenge history, streaks, statistics, achievements, and unlocked themes
  • Gameplay preferences, such as control style, tilt sensitivity, haptics, and selected theme

This information is stored locally using Apple's app-specific storage and is not transmitted to us. It may be removed if you delete the App, clear app data, or reset your device.

Device motion

If you enable tilt controls, the App processes device-motion readings to control the balance beam. Motion readings are used in real time on your device. We do not store them or transmit them to our servers.

3. Game Center

The App offers optional Apple Game Center features for player authentication, leaderboards, and achievements. If you use Game Center, the App may submit your Classic or Daily Challenge score, achievement completion, and interact with the Game Center player identity provided by Apple. Apple processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your Apple ID password or operate a separate copy of your Game Center account.

You can play the core game without using Game Center. Game Center availability and account controls are managed through your Apple device settings.

4. Advertising and consent

The App uses Google AdMob to display interstitial advertisements between gameplay rounds. Ads are not displayed during active gameplay. You can make a one-time in-app purchase to remove future advertisements.

Depending on your device, region, and consent choices, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process:

  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate coarse location
  • Device or app identifiers
  • Advertising data, such as ads shown to the device
  • Product interactions, such as app launches and ad interactions
  • Crash logs, performance data, and other diagnostics

Google may use this information to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising and related services. The App does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission in this version. Google may still use app-scoped identifiers and other data permitted by your consent choices and applicable platform rules.

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before loading ads. If a privacy-options entry point is required for you, you can reopen it from Info → Privacy Choices. For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchases and RevenueCat

The App offers a non-consumable purchase called Remove Ads Forever. Apple processes the payment through the App Store; we and RevenueCat do not receive your full payment-card details.

We use RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore access, prevent fraud, and determine whether the ad-free entitlement is active. RevenueCat processes purchase history, App Store transaction or receipt information, and an automatically generated anonymous app user identifier for app functionality and purchase analytics. We do not attach that anonymous identifier to a name, email address, or account profile. For more information, review RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

6. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly operate accounts for or directly collect contact information from children. Third-party services used by the App process limited data as described above and according to their own policies, consent requirements, and platform rules. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the method below.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is overwritten, the App is deleted, or the device data is erased. We do not maintain a server-side database of this local data, so we cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

Game Center data is controlled by Apple. Please use your Apple account and Game Center settings, or contact Apple, for requests concerning Game Center data.

Google and RevenueCat retain and delete information according to their respective policies and legal obligations. If you contact us about a privacy request, include only the minimum information needed for us to understand the request.

8. Security

We minimize our own data handling by keeping gameplay preferences and personal-best progress on the device and relying on established providers for ads and purchase validation. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. International users

The App may be available in multiple countries and regions. Third-party services, including Apple Game Center, Google AdMob, and RevenueCat, may process information using global infrastructure in accordance with their own privacy terms and applicable safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date and last-updated date at the top of this page will identify the latest version. Material changes will be reflected before the applicable App update is released.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests relating to Balance & Chill, open a privacy support request.
