Quick troubleshooting

Motion controls are unavailable or not responding

Motion controls require a physical iPhone or iPad with device-motion sensors. They may be unavailable in the Simulator. Make sure TILT is enabled in the game, hold your device in portrait orientation, then restart the app if the reading does not reset.

Game Center does not sign in

Open iOS Settings and verify that you are signed in to Game Center. Check your internet connection, then close and reopen Balance & Chill. You can still play with the LEFT and RIGHT buttons while Game Center is unavailable.

My leaderboard score has not appeared

Leaderboard submission requires an authenticated Game Center account and an internet connection. Apple may take a short time to update leaderboard results. Your local personal best remains available on the device.

My personal best was reset

The local best score is stored on your device. Deleting the app, resetting the device, or clearing app data may remove it. Scores already accepted by Game Center remain subject to Apple's Game Center service.

The game has no sound

The current version focuses on haptic and visual feedback. Confirm that system haptics are enabled if impacts are not felt. Haptics may behave differently on unsupported devices.

How do I control the beam?

Tap LEFT or RIGHT to apply counter-torque to the beam. On supported devices, enable TILT and gently rotate the device. Open INFO to tune tilt sensitivity or recenter the neutral position. Small, early corrections are more effective than large movements.

How do Daily Challenge and streaks work?

Select DAILY near the top of the game. Every player receives the same deterministic challenge for the current UTC day. Completing at least one Daily round counts that day toward your streak; missed days reset the current streak.

Where are my statistics, achievements, and themes?

Tap PROGRESS at the bottom of the game. Progress is stored locally on your device. Eligible Game Center achievements are also reported when you are signed in and online.

System requirements

  • iOS or iPadOS 15.6 or later
  • Portrait orientation
  • Internet access for Game Center, ads, purchases, and privacy consent services
  • A Game Center account is optional for normal gameplay

Contact

For bugs, feedback, accessibility questions, or privacy requests, use our public support request form on GitHub. Do not include passwords, Apple ID credentials, or other sensitive personal information.
