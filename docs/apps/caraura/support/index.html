CarAura Support

Need help? Email kb.technologies.support@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that failed. Avoid sending private photos, payment details or passwords.

Add a widget

Open Widgets, customize a template and save it to My Widgets. Add CarAura Dashboard using the iPhone Home Screen widget picker. CarPlay widgets require iOS 26 or later and a compatible vehicle. On iPhone, open Settings → General → CarPlay → your vehicle → Widgets and add CarAura. Vehicle layouts and refresh timing are controlled by Apple and the vehicle.

Lyrics, sounds and video

Timed lyrics use your own LRC text. Import matching audio in Lyrics, or try the original CarAura Demo, then choose Add to CarPlay to start a Live Activity on compatible iOS 26+ dashboards. Imported audio supports locked-phone playback. For audio playing in another app, keep CarAura open; if updates pause, reopen it to resume. Choose Stop CarPlay Lyrics to remove the activity. No commercial lyric library is provided. Sounds can be previewed/exported and configured with your own Shortcuts automations. TV plays your own HTTPS stream and supports compatible AirPlay routes while parked; CarAura does not provide channels, streaming subscriptions or video through CarPlay.

Temperature on a widget

In the widget editor, enable Show Temperature, choose °C or °F, and allow approximate location. Refresh Weather fetches current Apple Weather data. The widget uses the latest observation from CarAura; it does not continuously track your route. Readings older than one hour are hidden. If weather is unavailable, check network and location permission and try again. Apple Weather source attribution appears in the editor.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases in CarAura's paywall or Settings with the Apple Account that purchased Pro. Manage or cancel subscriptions at Apple subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. For refund requests, use Apple's refund service.
