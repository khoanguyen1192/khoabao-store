Privacy Policy

Updated November 9, 2026 · CarAura 1.0 · KB Technologies

Your designs stay on your device

CarAura stores widget designs, selected photos, messages, imported timed lyrics, streaming-source URLs and preferences on your device. The active design is shared with CarAura's widget extension through the same App Group. Selected photos are accessed through Apple's photo picker. CarAura does not upload these files to a CarAura server or operate a user-account service.

Features you choose to use

Apple Music permission lets CarAura read the selected track and playback position. Microphone access is requested when you choose song recognition, which uses Apple's recognition services. Streaming and AirPlay connect to the source or destination you choose; those operators may receive network and playback requests, including your IP address. Apple processes subscriptions and payments. CarAura does not receive payment-card details. Optional drive reminders are scheduled locally.

Optional local weather

When you enable temperature on a widget, CarAura asks for location permission while the app is in use. Approximate location is sufficient; iOS controls the accuracy of the location you allow. The location is sent to Apple Weather to fetch current conditions; CarAura does not save coordinates or build location history. Only the latest temperature, condition symbol, observation time and required attribution are shared locally with the widget. Refresh occurs when you open CarAura or choose Refresh Weather. Readings older than one hour are hidden. Disable the temperature option or location permission to stop new lookups. Apple processes requests under its privacy policy.

Subscription verification

RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription history, product identifiers, transaction information, an anonymous app user identifier and technical network information to verify CarAura Pro access, restore purchases and maintain subscription status. It does not receive your photos, widget messages, lyrics or streaming-source contents. Apple processes payments; neither CarAura nor RevenueCat receives your payment-card details. See RevenueCat privacy information. Subscription records may remain with Apple and RevenueCat after you uninstall the app as needed for restoration and their legal retention obligations.

Optional analytics and configuration

Usage analytics are off by default. If you enable them in Settings, Google Firebase Analytics processes app interactions, including a content-free widget-saved event, and technical information under Google's policies. Your photo, widget message, lyrics and playlist contents are not included in custom analytics events. Turning analytics off stops future analytics collection; it does not recall previously processed data. Firebase Remote Config requests configuration and processes country/language codes, time zone, OS version, app identifiers and technical installation/network information even when analytics is off.

CarAura includes Google on-device conversion measurement for event data, separately from showing ads. Ad conversion measurement is off by default and requires both usage analytics and the separate ad measurement option in Settings. Google processes consented event and technical information to measure ad effectiveness. We do not send an email, phone number, your photos, lyrics or widget text for matching. Personalized advertising remains disabled. CarAura does not display ads or request the advertising identifier. We do not sell your personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. See Firebase privacy information and Apple's privacy policy. Providers may process information outside your country and retain it under their published policies.

Your choices and retention

Delete saved widgets in My Widgets, remove imported content, turn off analytics or reminders in Settings, and change media permissions in iPhone Settings. Uninstalling removes local app data subject to Apple's backup and shared-container behavior. This does not cancel a subscription or delete Apple's transaction records. Manage subscriptions in your App Store account settings. For privacy questions or requests, contact kb.technologies.support@gmail.com; do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive media.

Changes

CarAura is a general-audience personalization app. We update this policy when its data practices change. The current policy will be posted here.

Tiếng Việt

Thiết kế, ảnh đã chọn, lời nhắn, tệp lời bài hát và nguồn phát được lưu trên thiết bị. Widget đang dùng được chia sẻ với tiện ích của chính CarAura. Phân tích sử dụng mặc định tắt; bạn có thể bật hoặc tắt trong Cài đặt. Firebase Remote Config vẫn có thể xử lý thông tin kỹ thuật để tải cấu hình. Quyền Apple Music và micro chỉ phục vụ thao tác bạn chọn. Khi bật nhiệt độ, vị trí được gửi tới Apple Weather; iOS quyết định độ chính xác theo quyền bạn cho phép; CarAura không lưu tọa độ hay lịch sử vị trí. Chỉ số đo mới nhất được lưu cục bộ và ẩn sau một giờ. Bạn có thể tắt nhiệt độ hoặc quyền vị trí. RevenueCat xử lý lịch sử mua hàng, thông tin giao dịch và mã ứng dụng ẩn danh để xác minh, khôi phục quyền Pro; dịch vụ không nhận ảnh, lời bài hát hay nội dung widget. Đo lường chuyển đổi quảng cáo của Google mặc định tắt và chỉ hoạt động khi bạn đồng ý ở cả mục phân tích sử dụng và mục đo lường quảng cáo riêng. App không cá nhân hóa quảng cáo, không yêu cầu mã quảng cáo và không gửi email hay số điện thoại để đối sánh. Apple xử lý thanh toán; nguồn phát/AirPlay bạn chọn xử lý kết nối tương ứng. Bạn có thể xóa widget và nội dung đã nhập, đổi quyền hoặc gỡ app. Gỡ app không hủy gói đăng ký. Liên hệ email hỗ trợ để hỏi về quyền riêng tư.
