Privacy Policy

Your sketches stay yours.

Updated November 22, 2026 · English / Tiếng Việt

Local artwork

FlipBookStudio stores drawings, imported reference images, projects, working drafts and preferences on your device. We do not provide an artwork account or cloud drawing service. The photo picker shares only the images you select. Saving exports to Photos requires your permission. Sharing sends the file to the destination you choose.

Optional diagnostics

Diagnostics are off by default. If you enable them in Settings, Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process app interactions, screen identifiers, export format and frame counts, crash reports and technical app/device information. We do not include artwork, project names, imported photos or entered text in these events. Automatic Firebase screen reporting is disabled. Advertising identifiers (IDFA and IDFV) are not collected by our Analytics configuration.

The app includes Google's on-device conversion measurement framework with Analytics. It does not display ads, collect email addresses or phone numbers for ad matching, or enable advertising consent. No Google Ads campaign or conversion import is implied by installing this framework. You can turn diagnostics off in Settings; this stops future optional collection, resets local Analytics identifiers and deletes unsent crash reports. It does not erase reports already received by Google.

Purchases

Apple handles payments. RevenueCat processes a generated app-user identifier, product and transaction information, device/app information and entitlement status to verify and restore the optional Creator purchase. We do not receive payment-card details. Creator is a one-time purchase, not a renewing subscription. Purchase verification can contact Apple and RevenueCat even when optional diagnostics are off. Network providers may receive IP addresses and request/security metadata.

Retention and requests

Local projects stay until you delete them or remove the app. Device/iCloud backups follow your Apple settings. Exported copies belong to their selected destinations. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their own policies. Google retains transmitted diagnostics under its service settings and policies. Support receives the email address and information you send. To request help or deletion of support information, contact kb.technologies.support@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, payment details or private artwork unless needed and you choose to share it.

Providers

See Apple Privacy, Firebase Privacy and RevenueCat Privacy. Providers may process data outside your country. We do not sell personal information. The app is a general creative tool and is not specifically directed to children. Changes to our practices will be recorded on this page.

Chẩn đoán mặc định tắt. Nếu bật trong Cài đặt, Firebase Analytics và Crashlytics xử lý thao tác, tên màn hình cố định, định dạng xuất, số khung, báo cáo lỗi và thông tin kỹ thuật ứng dụng/thiết bị. Sự kiện không chứa tranh, tên dự án, ảnh nhập hay nội dung bạn gõ. Cấu hình Analytics không thu IDFA/IDFV. Framework đo chuyển đổi trên thiết bị của Google không hiển thị quảng cáo, không thu email/số điện thoại để đối sánh và không bật đồng ý quảng cáo. Việc tích hợp không đồng nghĩa đã chạy chiến dịch hay nhập chuyển đổi Google Ads.

Tắt chẩn đoán sẽ dừng thu thập tùy chọn trong tương lai, đặt lại định danh Analytics cục bộ và xóa báo cáo lỗi chưa gửi; không xóa dữ liệu Google đã nhận. Creator mua một lần, không tự gia hạn. Chúng tôi không nhận số thẻ. Nhà cung cấp mạng có thể xử lý IP và thông tin bảo mật yêu cầu.
