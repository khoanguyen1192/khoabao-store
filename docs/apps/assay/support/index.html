“It will not give me a year”

That is the app working as intended. A date letter was reused every twenty to twenty-five years, and the published tables record some cycles with the same letterform and the same punch outline. When two or more cycles match everything you can see, Assay names them and stops rather than picking one. Adding the sovereign’s-head duty mark (struck only between 1784 and 1890) very often settles it.

“My piece is Scottish or Irish”

This release carries the full date-letter tables for London, Birmingham and Sheffield only. Edinburgh, Glasgow, Dublin and Chester marks are shown so you can recognise them, but they cannot be dated yet.

“What is it worth?”

Assay will not answer that, on purpose. No app can source a reliable sold price from a photograph, and the ones that print a figure are guessing. What Assay does instead is show what each selling route would actually net you from a given asking price, so you can compare routes rather than chase a number.

Restoring a purchase

Settings → Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that bought them.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling during a free trial costs nothing.

Deleting your data

Everything lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
