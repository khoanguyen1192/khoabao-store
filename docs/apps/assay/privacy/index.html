Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 15, 2026

Assay has no account and no server. Your photographs, your saved objects and your notes stay on your device and are never uploaded. Assay sends us nothing about you.

1. What Assay stores, and where

The objects you save, the photographs attached to them, the room and triage labels you assign and the notes you write are stored in app-specific storage on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the App removes this data. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete anything, because we never had it.

2. Camera and photo library

Assay asks for the camera so you can photograph the punches on a piece, and for the photo library so you can choose a photograph you already have. Both are optional — you can type what you see instead and every feature still works. Images you supply are read on the device to look for lettering; they are never uploaded, and Assay does not write to your photo library.

3. No analytics, no tracking

This version contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier and no tracking of any kind. We do not know what you photograph, what you identify or what you own.

4. Purchases

Assay Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes the payment and tells the App whether the purchase is active. We do not operate a receipt server and we do not use a third-party purchase platform. We never see your name, email address, Apple Account or payment details. Apple’s handling of purchases is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Links out

Assay contains links to this website and to Apple’s standard licence terms. Opening one hands you to your browser and to that site’s own privacy practices.

6. Children and international users

Assay is a reference utility for antiques and is not directed to children. It may be distributed internationally. Because no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no international transfer of your data by us.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this policy before releasing a version that materially changes these practices. For questions, use the support route below. Do not include passwords, Apple Account credentials or payment information in a support request.
