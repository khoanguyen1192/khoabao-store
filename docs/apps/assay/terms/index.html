What Assay is

A reference tool. It reads published hallmark, patent and registration tables and tells you which entries match what you describe. It is not an appraisal, not an authentication, and not a valuation. It does not state what an object is worth and it does not judge whether an object is genuine.

Accuracy

The tables are transcriptions of published references (Jackson, 1921; Chaffers, 1922; USPTO and UK registers) and of fee schedules read in September 2026. Transcriptions can contain errors, published tables themselves disagree in places, and fees change. Check anything that matters against a primary source or a qualified person before you act on it.

The selling calculator

The figures it shows are arithmetic on published fee schedules applied to a price you enter. They are estimates, not offers, not predictions and not financial advice.
