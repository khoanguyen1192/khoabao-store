How many skeins?

Weigh the gauge swatch you already made, type in the two numbers off the yarn label, and Skein tells you how much the finished piece will weigh, how many yards that is, and how many skeins to buy — with the working shown.

No account, no server, no analytics. Everything is arithmetic on your phone. The only thing Skein asks of you is a kitchen scale.

Why weigh

Yarn is sold by weight and every label says how many yards are in a gram. A swatch made with your hook, your yarn and your hands fixes how heavy a square inch of your fabric is; scale it to the size you are making and the answer is yours, not an average from someone else's pattern. We tested the usual shortcut first — estimating from size and yarn weight alone missed by 18% on a typical published pattern and by nearly half on the worst one.
