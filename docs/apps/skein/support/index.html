Support

Questions, problems, or a count that came out wrong — write to khoanguyen1192@gmail.com. Include the project size, the yarn's yards and grams, and the swatch size and weight, and we can check the arithmetic with you.

The number seems too high or too low

Check three things: the swatch size is measured on the finished, relaxed swatch (not stretched); the scale reads grams, not ounces; and the yards and grams are for one skein as printed on the label. A 4 × 4 in swatch in worsted yarn usually weighs between 8 and 14 g — if yours reads 1 g or 100 g, the scale or the units are the problem.

I changed hook or yarn

Make and weigh a new swatch. Gauge changes with hook, tension and yarn, and the calculation assumes the piece matches the swatch. The "Swap the yarn" screen is for the same gauge in a different yarn; when the weight class differs, swatch again.

I have no scale

Use "Estimate from the yarn weight instead" on the swatch screen. You will get a range, not a number — it is wide on purpose — and Skein will ask you to weigh before you buy.

Stash says I am short but I own the yarn

Stash lines are matched by yarn name and colour name. Make sure the names on the stash entry match the project's yarn and colour spelling (capitalisation does not matter). Two dye lots of the same colour are kept as two lines on purpose.

Restoring a purchase

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not to a Skein account, because Skein has no accounts.
