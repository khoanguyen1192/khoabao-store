1. What Skein does

Skein computes yarn requirements for crochet and knitting projects from a project size, the yards and grams printed on a yarn label, and the weight of a gauge swatch. It can also show a rough estimate without a swatch, labelled as a range.

2. Accuracy

The swatch-based figure is arithmetic on the numbers you enter. It assumes the finished piece is made with the same yarn, hook and tension as the swatch. The no-swatch estimate is derived from published patterns and is shown as a range because it is not precise. Skein adds a purchase margin, but the developer does not guarantee that any figure will be sufficient for your project. Check your numbers, keep your receipt, and buy from one dye lot.
