Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 15, 2026

Skein has no account and no server. Your projects, yarns and stash are stored on your device and are never uploaded. Skein sends us nothing about you.

1. What Skein stores, and where

The projects you calculate, the yarns you enter and — with Skein Pro — the skeins in your stash are saved in app-specific storage on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the App removes this data. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete anything, because we never had it.

2. No analytics, no tracking

This version contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier and no tracking of any kind. We do not know how many projects you calculate or what you own.

3. No permissions

Skein does not ask for access to your photos, camera, location, contacts or notifications.

4. Purchases

Skein Pro is sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes the payment and tells the App whether the purchase is active. We do not operate a receipt server and we do not use a third-party purchase platform. We never see your name, email address, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Children and international users

Skein is a craft utility and is not directed to children. It may be distributed internationally. Because no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no international transfer of your data by us.

6. Changes and contact

We will update this policy before releasing a version that materially changes these practices. For questions, use the support route below. Do not include passwords, Apple Account credentials or payment information in a support request.
