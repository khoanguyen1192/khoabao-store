Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 5, 2026

Short version: SwingPlan has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated server. Club measurements and build sheets stay on your device.

1. Scope

This policy explains how SwingPlan (“the App”), developed by Khoa Nguyen (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information on iPhone and iPad.

2. Data handling

We do not directly collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information through the App. The App stores club measurements, targets, modeled results, physical calibration readings, and app preferences locally in Apple-provided app storage on your device.

You can delete individual build sheets or all local build sheets inside the App. Deleting the App may also remove its local data, subject to your device backup settings.

3. Purchases

The optional Workshop purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process transaction and account information under Apple’s own privacy policy. The App reads Apple-signed entitlement information only to unlock purchased features and restore access.

4. Network access and links

The free planning features work without a developer-operated backend. Network access may be used by Apple for App Store purchase and restore services, and when you choose to open policy or support links.

5. Children

The App is a golf club planning utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

6. Security and retention

Local records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App. Because we do not maintain a server-side copy, we cannot retrieve or delete those records remotely.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The date above identifies the current version.

8. Contact

Open a privacy support request. Please do not include passwords, Apple ID credentials, payment details, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
