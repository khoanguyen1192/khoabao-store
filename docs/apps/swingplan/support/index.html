Open a support request

Why does my physical result differ?

Recheck total weight and balance point, confirm the reference edge used for both measurements, and measure the assembled club on a physical Lorythmic scale. Component tolerances and mass distribution can make simplified what-if models differ from the final build.

How do I restore Workshop?

Open Settings in SwingPlan, tap Restore Purchases, and use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. A network connection to Apple’s App Store services is required.

Where are build sheets stored?

Build sheets are stored locally on your device. SwingPlan does not operate an account or cloud backend. You can delete a sheet from its menu or delete all sheets in Settings.

System requirements

  • iOS or iPadOS 17.0 or later
  • iPhone or iPad
  • Internet access only for App Store purchase/restore and external links

Measurement safety

SwingPlan is a planning utility. Use removable lead tape and remeasure before permanent modification. Follow lead-product handling instructions, wash hands after handling lead, keep it away from children, and consult a qualified club builder when uncertain.
