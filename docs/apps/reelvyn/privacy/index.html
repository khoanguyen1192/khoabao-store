Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026 · Beta policy 1.1

Pre-release service. Reelvyn is currently available only for controlled testing. This page describes the beta service. WaveSpeed has confirmed permission for the scoped commercial integration. Public AI availability still awaits production acceptance and clarification of model-specific face/voice handling, upstream providers and deletion limits. Automated provider-task cleanup is implemented for the beta, but its production rollout has not yet been verified; contact Support for provider deletion requests. Use authorized synthetic sample media during this pilot. This notice is not a guarantee that every feature is available.
AI creation uses cloud services. The images, video, audio and prompts you choose for a generation are sent to the provider named in its confirmation screen after your permission.

1. Who operates Reelvyn

Reelvyn: AI Photo & Video is operated by Nguyen Dinh Bao Khoa, an individual. Questions and privacy requests: kb.technologies.support@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

  • Account: email, user ID, display name and sign-in information from Apple, Google or email authentication. Apple may supply a private relay email. Your sign-in credentials and sessions support access to your own projects.
  • Creative content: selected photos, face references, videos, recordings, prompts, captions, project settings and generated results. We store file metadata such as format, size and duration.
  • Transactions: Apple transaction and product IDs, purchase status, account-linked purchase token and credit ledger. Apple handles payment; Reelvyn does not receive your full card number.
  • Operations: task status, model, costs, timings, error codes and account-linked workflow events, used to operate the app, prevent duplicate charges, investigate failures and protect the service. Hosting providers receive network information such as IP addresses.
  • Support: your email and any information you choose to include in a message.

3. Faces, voices and AI providers

Face swap, portrait editing, talking portraits and lip sync process facial or voice content to transform your selected media. Provider processing may include extracting facial characteristics or other information considered biometric or sensitive under applicable law. Do not upload another person’s face or voice without permission.

Only the inputs needed for the selected task are sent to its named provider. Reelvyn does not need your entire photo library, contacts or precise location for these features. You can decline generation consent; the task will not be submitted.

Reelvyn does not itself train a model on your content. This does not mean every provider excludes training. Black Forest Labs’ public policy allows use of inputs and outputs for model improvement/training and describes an opt-out request. Other providers’ retention, improvement and deletion practices depend on their policies and applicable model/service terms. We do not promise zero retention or no training across all providers. Review the linked policies before sending sensitive material.

WaveSpeed-specific information, confirmed on October 5, 2026: WaveSpeed states that it does not use submitted or generated customer content to train or improve its models. It cannot confirm the practices of third-party closed-source providers. We have not established whether those providers train on your inputs, or whether face embeddings or biometric templates are created or retained. WaveSpeed has not provided a DPA; subprocessors and backup deletion remain unconfirmed. Do not infer a no-training guarantee across the entire model supply chain.

4. Storage, access and international processing

Supabase provides authentication, database and private file storage. Railway hosts the API and background worker. AI providers process the selected generation inputs. Apple handles purchases and Apple sign-in; Google handles Google sign-in. A support email is processed by the email service.

Files are access-controlled for your account; temporary signed URLs allow authorized downloads and provider processing. A signed URL expiring does not delete its underlying file. Data may be processed outside your country. WaveSpeed reports infrastructure in Singapore, the European Union and the United States; this does not guarantee a fixed processing region for each task. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Retention and deletion

Saved projects and media remain available until you delete them or your account; Reelvyn currently does not automatically remove all media after a fixed number of days. Jobs and credit records support completion, recovery and accounting while the account remains active.

You can request account deletion from the account screen. Deletion runs in the background and can wait for active generation/export tasks or retry a failed storage or authentication operation. It removes account-owned records and files from Reelvyn’s active systems and revokes available Apple sign-in authorization. If deletion appears stalled, contact support.

Deleting a project or account does not automatically remove copies you saved to Photos/Files, content you shared elsewhere, Apple’s payment records or copies retained by providers or backups. Those copies follow the relevant service’s practices. Contact us for assistance with a provider-specific privacy request; we will explain what can be located and what remains outside our control.

WaveSpeed retention and task deletion: WaveSpeed reports that uploaded and generated media is retained for 7 days, cannot be removed earlier and is then automatically deleted. Task records and prompts are retained for at least 3 months unless deleted. When this beta cleanup workflow is enabled and you delete a project or account, Reelvyn queues a separate request to delete known WaveSpeed task records and retries temporary failures; this queue may retain your internal account ID and the provider task/model IDs until processing succeeds. Local account removal can finish while that request is pending. Running tasks cannot be canceled through the task-delete API, so the worker waits until they finish. A successful acknowledgement removes the task request/output data from provider task access, but does not establish early physical media or backup erasure. Task/model IDs, deletion status and billing history remain visible at the provider. For an ambiguous submission with no recovered task ID, automatic provider deletion is unavailable; contact support for assistance.

6. Your choices and requests

Use iOS media pickers to choose what to import. Saving a result to Photos requests add-only permission; you can change it in iOS Settings. Sharing opens the system share sheet and sends content only to the destination you select. The receiving app applies its own policy.

You may request access, correction, deletion or information about your data by contacting kb.technologies.support@gmail.com. Depending on your location, additional rights may include objecting to processing, restricting it, portability or complaining to a privacy authority. We may need proportionate verification of account ownership. Do not email passwords or full payment information.

7. Website, children and updates

This policy website does not add advertising trackers or a user account. Its hosting service may process connection logs. Reelvyn is a general creative tool, not a service directed to children. If you believe a child has supplied personal information without appropriate authorization, contact us. Eligibility restrictions for particular AI services also apply.

Material changes will be described in the updated policy and, where required, brought to your attention in the app. The date above identifies this version.

8. Provider policies

Supabase, Railway, Black Forest Labs, Runway, WaveSpeedAI, Apple and Google. These links describe the providers’ own practices; they do not replace Reelvyn’s responsibilities described here.

Quy trình tự động yêu cầu xóa bản ghi tác vụ tại provider đã được xây dựng cho beta, nhưng chưa xác minh triển khai production. Vui lòng liên hệ Support để yêu cầu xóa tại provider.

3. Khuôn mặt, giọng nói và nhà cung cấp AI

Ghép mặt, chỉnh chân dung, avatar nói và lip sync xử lý nội dung mặt hoặc giọng để biến đổi media bạn chọn. Nhà cung cấp có thể trích xuất đặc điểm khuôn mặt hoặc thông tin được coi là dữ liệu sinh trắc học/nhạy cảm theo pháp luật áp dụng. Không tải mặt hay giọng người khác lên nếu chưa có sự cho phép.

Chỉ các đầu vào cần cho tác vụ được gửi tới nhà cung cấp đã nêu. Các tính năng này không cần toàn bộ thư viện ảnh, danh bạ hay vị trí chính xác. Bạn có thể từ chối consent; tác vụ sẽ không được gửi.

Reelvyn không tự huấn luyện model bằng nội dung của bạn. Điều này không có nghĩa mọi nhà cung cấp đều loại trừ huấn luyện. Chính sách công khai của Black Forest Labs cho phép dùng đầu vào/đầu ra để cải thiện hoặc huấn luyện model và hướng dẫn yêu cầu từ chối. Việc lưu giữ, cải thiện hệ thống và xóa dữ liệu của nhà cung cấp khác phụ thuộc chính sách và điều khoản model/dịch vụ áp dụng. Chúng tôi không cam kết mọi provider đều không lưu dữ liệu hoặc không huấn luyện. Hãy đọc các chính sách được liên kết trước khi gửi nội dung nhạy cảm.

Họ không thể xác nhận thực hành của nhà cung cấp model đóng phía sau. Chúng tôi chưa xác minh việc các nhà cung cấp đó dùng đầu vào để huấn luyện hoặc tạo/lưu embedding khuôn mặt hay mẫu sinh trắc học. WaveSpeed chưa cung cấp DPA; đơn vị xử lý phụ và cách xóa backup còn chưa được xác nhận. Không suy ra cam kết không huấn luyện cho toàn bộ chuỗi model.
