RV route planning

Plan once.
Drive it on CarPlay.

RigPath: RV Safe Routes plans a route for your rig’s height, width, length, weight and propane, names the structure it avoided and the lowest clearance on the way, splits long trips into driving days, and guides you on CarPlay.

Routed for your rig

Enter your dimensions once. Every route is calculated with them, and the card explains the detour instead of hiding it.

Honest about data

Segments without restriction data are marked unverified. If the structure database can’t load, the App says so. Signs always win.

Yours, on your device

No account. Rig profile and trips stay on your phone; planning is free, guidance is Pro.
