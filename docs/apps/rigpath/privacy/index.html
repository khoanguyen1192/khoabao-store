Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 12, 2026

RigPath: RV Safe Routes does not require an account. Your rig profile and saved trips stay on your device. To plan a route the App must send your start point, destination and rig dimensions to a routing provider; while you drive, it uses your location on the device to follow the route.

1. What stays on your device

Your rig profile (height, width, length, weight, propane), your trips (names, coordinates, routes, day splits) and app preferences are stored in app-specific device storage. There is no cloud account or sync in this version. Deleting a trip or the App removes that local data; we cannot retrieve or remotely delete it.

2. Location

The App asks for location permission only when you start guidance. Your position is used on the device to show where you are on the route, announce the next manoeuvre and detect when you have left the route, including in the background during an active drive so guidance continues when the screen is off. Location is not stored beyond the current drive and is not sent to us. You can stop guidance at any time; the App then stops using location.

3. Route planning (TomTom)

When you plan a trip, the App sends the start and end coordinates you chose and your rig dimensions (height, width, length, weight, and whether you carry propane) to TomTom’s Routing API, which returns the route. TomTom processes that request under its privacy policy. The App does not send your name, device identifiers or trip history with the request.

4. Structure data (bridge heights and weight limits)

To check a route against known low bridges and weight limits, the App downloads small map tiles of restriction data for the area around your route from our static hosting. The tile request reveals which map area you are planning in, at roughly city scale; it does not include your exact points or your rig. The data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors (© OpenStreetMap, ODbL). Tiles may be cached on your device.

5. Maps and address search (Apple)

Map imagery and address search use Apple’s MapKit. Apple processes those requests under its privacy policy; we do not receive them.

6. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

RigPath Pro is an optional subscription sold through Apple’s App Store; Apple processes payment. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app-user identifier and purchase, entitlement and transaction/receipt information so the App can validate, restore and unlock your subscription. We do not attach that identifier to your name, email, rig or trips. RevenueCat’s handling is described in its Privacy Policy.

7. Analytics and diagnostics

This version does not send product analytics to any third-party service. Diagnostic events are written only to the device’s system log. If a future version adds analytics, this policy will be updated first and the events will never include coordinates, addresses, rig dimensions or purchase amounts.

8. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information and do not run an operational backend that stores your trips. TomTom, Apple and RevenueCat retain what they process under their own policies. Local data lives as long as you keep it on your device.

9. Children and international users

RigPath: RV Safe Routes is a driving-planning utility and is not directed to children. It may be distributed internationally; the providers above process the limited information described here on global infrastructure under their applicable terms and safeguards.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this policy before releasing a version that materially changes its data practices. For privacy questions, use the support route below. Do not include passwords, Apple ID credentials or payment information in a support request.
