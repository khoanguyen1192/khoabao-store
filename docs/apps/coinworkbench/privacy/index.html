Privacy Policy

Your collection, your records.

Privacy Policy · Updated September 29, 2026

What stays on your device

CoinWorkbench stores coin photos, notes, albums, research claims, source links and app preferences in local app storage. There is no CoinWorkbench account or cloud collection sync. Camera access is used only when you choose to photograph a coin; the photo picker lets you choose images to add.

AI photo analysis

With your separate in-app permission, you may send selected coin photos through our Firebase service for an identification suggestion or condition estimate. Gemini is the primary provider. OpenAI receives the selected photos only if Gemini has a retryable service failure. Our service is designed not to retain submitted photos or generated analysis; it keeps only a monthly usage total needed to enforce the service budget. You can withdraw this permission in Settings; withdrawing it prevents future AI photo-analysis requests but does not remove the local copies of your photos.

AI suggestions are research aids, not authentication, certification, an appraisal, or a guarantee of identity or grade. Google and OpenAI process data under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not use your submitted content to train our own models.

Connected services and purchases

Google Firebase provides app configuration and the secure server route for AI requests. RevenueCat provides subscription infrastructure and may process a generated app-user identifier, app/device information, product and transaction status, and entitlement requests. Apple processes payments. We do not receive payment-card details. Network providers may receive technical data such as IP address, request time and security information. We do not use advertising identifiers or sell personal information.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled in your Apple account. RevenueCat and Apple retain purchase and transaction information under their own policies so they can provide, restore and manage access.

Sharing and support

Exporting a report or using the share sheet sends the content you select to the destination you choose. That destination handles its own copy under its policies. If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you include. Please do not send payment details, passwords or unrelated personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting a record removes it from the active collection. Device or iCloud backups are controlled by your Apple backup settings. Exported copies, support emails and Apple transaction records are governed by the relevant recipient's retention practices.

For privacy or deletion requests concerning support information, email kb.technologies.support@gmail.com.

Providers and changes

See Apple Privacy, Firebase Privacy, RevenueCat Privacy, Gemini API Terms and OpenAI Privacy. These providers may process information outside your country. CoinWorkbench is intended for general collectors and is not directed specifically to children. We will update this page when our data practices change.
