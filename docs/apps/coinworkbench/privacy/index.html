Privacy Policy

Your collection, your records.

Privacy Policy · Updated September 26, 2026 · Current prerelease configuration

What stays on your device

CoinWorkbench stores coin photos, notes, albums, research claims, source links and app preferences in its local app storage. There is currently no cloud collection sync or app account. Camera access is used when you choose to photograph a coin; the photo picker lets you choose images to add. These records are not uploaded to our servers by the current prerelease build.

Connected services

Google Firebase Core initializes the app configuration. Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics collection, messaging auto-initialization and Remote Config are disabled in this configuration. RevenueCat initializes subscription infrastructure and may process a generated app-user identifier, app/device information and entitlement requests. Network services may receive technical information such as IP address and request time. We do not use advertising identifiers or sell personal information.

When purchases become available, Apple processes payments and RevenueCat processes product, transaction and subscription status to provide and restore access. We do not receive payment-card details. Purchases are not enabled in the current prerelease build.

AI processing

AI identification and condition analysis are currently disabled. The current prerelease build does not send your coin photos to an AI provider. Before enabling these services, we will update this policy to identify the provider, transmitted information and retention practices and provide the required in-app disclosure and choice.

Sharing and support

Exporting a report or using the share sheet sends the content you select to the destination you choose. That destination handles its own copy under its policies. If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you include. Please avoid sending payment details, passwords or unrelated personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting a record removes it from the active collection. Migration backups and older local copies may remain in the app container for recovery; removing the app removes that container. Device or iCloud backups are controlled by your Apple backup settings. Exported copies, support emails and Apple transaction records are not deleted by removing a coin or the app.

For privacy or deletion requests concerning information you sent to support, email kb.technologies.support@gmail.com. We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. Apple and RevenueCat maintain service and transaction information under their own retention policies.

Providers and changes

See Apple Privacy, Firebase Privacy and RevenueCat Privacy. These providers may process information outside your country. CoinWorkbench is intended for general collectors and is not directed specifically to children. We update this page when our data practices change.
