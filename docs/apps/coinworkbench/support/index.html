Support

Keep your research moving.

CoinWorkbench Support · Updated September 26, 2026

Need help? Email kb.technologies.support@gmail.com. Include “CoinWorkbench” in the subject. If email does not open, copy the address into your mail app.

Save your first coin

Open Collection and choose to create a research dossier manually, or use Scan to capture photos. Add a title and save. Open a saved coin and choose “Edit coin & research evidence” to update the same record. Unknown details can stay blank.

Photos and permissions

Use even light and keep the entire coin in frame. If camera access is denied, change the permission in iOS Settings or choose a photo from your library. You can continue entering research notes without camera access.

Sources and disagreements

Add the source behind a claim. If a date or other detail conflicts, keep both claims until you can explain the difference. An AI suggestion is not independent verification or professional certification.

Membership

AI services and purchases are not yet available in the current prerelease build. When available, restore purchases in Membership using your purchasing Apple account. Manage or cancel a subscription in Apple account subscriptions. Removing the app does not cancel it.

Report a problem

Tell us what you were doing, what happened, your app version and your iOS version. A screenshot is optional; remove personal information before sending it. Do not email passwords, payment-card details or API keys.

Protect your records

If the app reports a storage error, retain your existing installation and contact support before deleting data or reinstalling. Export important research when possible. See the Privacy Policy for local backups and deletion details.
