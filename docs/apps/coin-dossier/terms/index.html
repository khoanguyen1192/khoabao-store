Using Coin Dossier

Coin Dossier helps you organize and research coins. Keep only content you own or have permission to use. The app is licensed under Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement.

Research is not authentication

Photos, AI suggestions, condition studies and user-entered claims do not authenticate a coin, certify its grade or establish market value. Verify dates, mint marks, varieties, measurements and provenance against the physical coin and reliable sources. Obtain a qualified professional assessment for buying, selling, insurance or other financial decisions. Coin Dossier does not provide live market quotes.
