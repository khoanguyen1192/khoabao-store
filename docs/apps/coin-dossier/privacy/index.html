Privacy Policy

Your collection, your records.

Updated October 25, 2026

What stays on your device

Your coin photos, notes, collections, research claims, source links and preferences are stored in local app storage. Collection sync is not provided. Camera access is used when you choose to photograph a coin; the photo picker lets you choose images to add. You do not need to register or provide an email address to use Coin Dossier.

Optional AI photo analysis

After you separately consent in the app, selected coin photos and the analysis instructions are sent through Firebase to OpenAI for identification or condition suggestions. Our application server does not persist the photos, instructions or generated analysis. Results you save become part of your local records.

OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default, with exceptions described in its API data controls. Coin Dossier does not have a verified Zero Data Retention arrangement. API data is not used for model training by default. Do not submit photos containing personal or sensitive information you do not want to share.

You can withdraw permission in Settings. This stops subsequent analysis requests; it does not recall content already sent to a provider or delete your local records. AI suggestions are research aids, not authentication, certification, an appraisal or a guarantee of identity or grade.

Service identity, security and usage

Firebase provides configuration, app integrity checks, anonymous authentication and the AI server route. When you use AI, an automatically generated authentication identifier links the request to your RevenueCat subscription entitlement. No name or email is required. Our server retains a hashed identifier, request IDs, timestamps, rate counters and monthly budget totals to prevent replay and limit usage. These records contain no coin photos or analysis content. Technical service logs can include request timing, errors and network information such as IP addresses.

Purchases

RevenueCat processes an app-user identifier, app/device information, subscription products, transaction information and entitlement status. Apple processes payments. We do not receive payment-card details. The app does not use advertising identifiers, targeted advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do not sell personal information.

Sharing, retention and deletion

In Settings, choose “Delete AI service account” to remove your anonymous Firebase authentication, associated per-user usage/request records and RevenueCat customer record. This preserves your local coin records and does not cancel an Apple subscription. A hash-only deletion marker expires after 24 hours and is then removed by Firestore's asynchronous TTL process; anonymous monthly budget totals and provider security logs are separate.

Exporting or sharing a report sends your selected content to the destination you choose. Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app; device and iCloud backups follow your Apple backup settings. Removing the app does not cancel an Apple subscription and may leave credentials in the system Keychain.

Security and subscription records are retained as needed to operate the service and prevent abuse, unless deletion is required or an applicable legal obligation requires retention. To request deletion of service records, anonymous authentication or support correspondence, contact kb.technologies.support@gmail.com. We may ask for information needed to locate the relevant record and verify your request. Support receives the email address and information you choose to send.

Providers and changes

See Apple Privacy, Firebase Privacy, RevenueCat Privacy and OpenAI Privacy. Coin Dossier is intended for general collectors and is not directed specifically to children. We update this page when our data practices change.
