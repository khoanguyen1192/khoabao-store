Privacy Policy

Your collection, your records.

Updated October 25, 2026

What stays on your device

Coin Dossier stores the coin photos, notes, collections, research claims, source links and preferences that you create in local app storage. The app does not require a Coin Dossier account or provide cloud collection sync. Camera access is used only when you choose to photograph a coin; the photo picker lets you choose images to add.

Optional AI photo analysis

After you separately consent in the app, you may send selected coin photos to our protected server route for an identification suggestion or condition-study aid. We use approved AI providers only to process the request. The service is designed not to retain submitted photos or generated analysis; it keeps a monthly usage total needed to enforce the service budget. You can withdraw consent in Settings; this prevents future AI analysis but does not remove the local photos you keep.

AI suggestions are research aids, not authentication, certification, an appraisal, or a guarantee of identity or grade. We do not use submitted content to train our own models.

Connected services and purchases

Firebase provides app configuration, app integrity checks and the secure server route for the optional AI feature. RevenueCat provides subscription infrastructure and may process an app-user identifier, app/device information, product and transaction status, and entitlement requests. Apple processes payments. We do not receive payment-card details. Network providers may receive technical data such as IP address, request time and security information. We do not use advertising identifiers or sell personal information.

Sharing, retention and support

Exporting a report or using the share sheet sends the content you select to the destination you choose. If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you include. Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app; device and iCloud backups are controlled by your Apple backup settings. For a privacy or deletion request concerning support information, email kb.technologies.support@gmail.com.

Providers and changes

See Apple Privacy, Firebase Privacy and RevenueCat Privacy. Coin Dossier is intended for general collectors and is not directed specifically to children. We will update this page when our data practices change.
