Support

Keep your research moving.

Coin Dossier Support · Updated October 25, 2026

Need help? Email kb.technologies.support@gmail.com. Include “Coin Dossier” in the subject.

Save your first coin

Create a dossier manually or use Scan to capture photos. Add a title and save. You can return to the same dossier to update observations and research evidence. Unknown details can remain blank.

Photos and permissions

Use even light and keep the entire coin in frame. If camera access is denied, change it in iOS Settings or choose a photo from your library. You can continue entering notes without camera access.

Optional AI analysis

AI photo analysis is optional and asks for separate consent before selected photos are sent for a research suggestion. You can withdraw that consent in Settings. A suggestion is not independent verification or professional certification.

Membership

Restore purchases in Membership using the Apple account that made the purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription in Apple account subscriptions. Removing the app does not cancel it.

Report a problem

Tell us what you were doing, what happened, your app version and iOS version. A screenshot is optional; remove personal information before sending it. Do not email passwords, payment-card details or API keys.
