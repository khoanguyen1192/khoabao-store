Privacy Policy · Updated September 23, 2026 · Applies to Nudge 1.0

Nudge stores your current draft and session on your device. AI processing is optional and requires your separate in-app permission.

1. Data stored on your device

Nudge stores your draft, current session, timer state, preferences and content-free local usage events in protected app storage. A widget may receive a short current-action snapshot for re-entry. Nudge does not maintain a task backlog or send local analytics to an advertising service.

Voice input is converted to editable text using Apple speech recognition when available. Nudge does not save audio. Apple’s handling of speech recognition is governed by Apple’s policies.

2. Optional AI help

If you choose “Allow AI with OpenAI,” Nudge sends the task you selected, your chosen friction, recent steps in the current session and a language setting through our Firebase Cloud Function to OpenAI’s API to return one small action. Nudge sends this only after your consent and stops future AI requests when you turn AI off. We use OpenAI’s Responses API with provider-side storage disabled for these requests; OpenAI may still retain data as described in its API data policy, including for abuse prevention.

Our function does not log or store task text, voice audio, generated action text or raw provider responses. It keeps one-way hashed identifiers, local-day counters, turn counts and timestamps only to apply the free allowance and abuse controls. These quota records are deleted on a best-effort basis after 35 days.

3. Purchases and App Check

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat processes transaction and subscription status, product identifiers and a generated app-user identifier to provide and restore Pro access. Nudge’s server checks that entitlement using a server-only RevenueCat key. Firebase App Check receives app and device-integrity attestations to protect the AI endpoint. These providers and network operators may receive technical information such as IP address, device/app version and request time. Nudge does not receive payment-card details and does not use advertising identifiers.

4. Sharing and retention

We share only what is needed with Apple, OpenAI, Google Firebase and RevenueCat to run the features described above. Their processing may occur outside your country. See the policies of Apple, Firebase, RevenueCat and OpenAI above. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

5. Your choices

You can decline or revoke AI permission in Nudge Settings. You can delete locally stored Nudge data in Settings; this cannot recall content already sent to a provider, cancel an Apple subscription, or erase Apple’s transaction records. Manage subscriptions through App Store settings. For privacy questions or a deletion request, email kb.technologies.support@gmail.com; do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive task content.

6. Children and changes

Nudge is a general productivity app and is not directed to children. We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The current version will always be posted here.
