Support

Email kb.technologies.support@gmail.com with your question. Include your Salii version and iOS version; please leave out passwords, payment details and private task text.

AI help is not available

AI is optional. Check Salii Settings to make sure AI is allowed, then try again on a connection. If it is temporarily unavailable, Salii can still offer a local starting step. Turn AI off at any time to stop future AI requests.

Restore a purchase

Open Salii Pro and choose Restore Purchases. Purchases are associated with your Apple Account. If Apple shows an active subscription but Salii does not restore it, contact us with the product name and purchase date; do not send receipts containing personal details unless we ask for them.

Delete local Salii data

In Salii Settings, choose Delete my data and confirm. This removes the local draft, current session and preferences. It does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple’s purchase record.

Crisis or urgent safety support

Salii is not an emergency or crisis service. If you may harm yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a crisis service in your area now.
