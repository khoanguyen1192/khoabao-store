Support

Email kb.technologies.support@gmail.com with your question. Include your Nudge version and iOS version; please leave out passwords, payment details and private task text.

AI help is not available

AI is optional. Check Nudge Settings to make sure AI is allowed, then try again on a connection. If it is temporarily unavailable, Nudge can still offer a local starting step. Turn AI off at any time to stop future AI requests.

Restore a purchase

Open Nudge Pro and choose Restore Purchases. Purchases are associated with your Apple Account. If Apple shows an active subscription but Nudge does not restore it, contact us with the product name and purchase date; do not send receipts containing personal details unless we ask for them.

Delete local Nudge data

In Nudge Settings, choose Delete my data and confirm. This removes the local draft, current session and preferences. It does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple’s purchase record.

Crisis or urgent safety support

Nudge is not an emergency or crisis service. If you may harm yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a crisis service in your area now.
