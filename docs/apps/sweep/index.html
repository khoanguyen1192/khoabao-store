iPhone · iPad

The photos you took twice.

Sweep compares your camera roll on your device, groups the shots that are the same moment, keeps the sharpest one — and tells you why it chose it. Five hundred swipes become a couple of hundred taps.

Everything is compared on your iPhone. No photo is uploaded. Nothing is removed until you confirm each group, and anything removed sits in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

What it finds

Identical copies of the same file · bursts and near-identical retries · screenshots you never opened again · videos large enough to matter.

What it will not claim

Sweep tidies your photo and video library. It cannot clear app data or system files — no iPhone app can, whatever the App Store says. And it does not pretend your storage is free the moment you tap delete: iOS reclaims that space when Recently Deleted is emptied, and Sweep says so instead of showing you a number your Settings screen will contradict.
