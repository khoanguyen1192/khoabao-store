iPhone · iPad

Review clutter. Keep control.

Lessbyte compares your camera roll on your device, groups similar shots, and helps you choose what stays. It also gives you an optional Gmail review for promotions and old mail. Every change stays reviewable: photos go to Recently Deleted, and mail goes to Gmail Trash only after you confirm.

Everything is compared on your iPhone. No photo is uploaded. Nothing is removed until you confirm each group, and anything removed sits in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

What it finds

Identical copies of the same file · bursts and near-identical retries · screenshots you never opened again · videos large enough to matter.

Optional Gmail review

Connect your own Google account only when you choose. Lessbyte counts selected Gmail categories and displays a bounded review of sender and subject metadata. It does not read message bodies, sell Gmail data or send it to Lessbyte servers. You choose any move to Gmail Trash.

What it will not claim

Lessbyte tidies your photo and video library. It cannot clear app data or system files — no iPhone app can, whatever the App Store says. And it does not pretend your storage is free the moment you tap delete: iOS reclaims that space when Recently Deleted is emptied, and Lessbyte says so instead of showing you a number your Settings screen will contradict.
