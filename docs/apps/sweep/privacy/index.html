Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 14, 2026

Sweep has no account and no server. Your photos are compared on your iPhone and are never uploaded. Sweep sends us nothing about you, your library, or what you delete.

1. Your photos never leave the device

Sweep reads your photo and video library only after you grant access, and only to compare items with each other. Comparison runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's on-device frameworks. No photo, video, thumbnail, or derived description of an image is transmitted anywhere.

Sweep deliberately does not download photos that exist only in iCloud. Items whose originals are not on the device are reported as not compared rather than fetched over your connection.

2. Sweep collects no analytics

This version contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, and no tracking of any kind. We do not know how many scans you run, what Sweep finds, or what you choose to remove.

3. What Sweep stores

Your review decisions, settings, and the summary of your last scan are stored in app-specific storage on your device. Deleting the App removes them. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete anything, because we never had it.

4. Purchases

Sweep Pro is sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes the payment and tells the App whether the purchase is active. We do not operate a receipt server and we do not use a third-party purchase platform. We never see your name, email address, Apple Account, or payment details.

Apple's handling of purchases is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

4b. Sweep Pro background check and notifications

With Sweep Pro, iOS may wake Sweep briefly in the background to compare photos added since your last sweep. This runs on your device exactly like a foreground scan: nothing is uploaded, and nothing is removed. If repeats are found, Sweep may show one local notification. Local notifications are generated on your device and go through no server of ours. You can turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.

5. Deletion and recovery

When you confirm a removal, Sweep asks iOS to move those items to Recently Deleted, where iOS keeps them for 30 days. Sweep never empties that album and cannot permanently erase anything.

6. Children and international users

Sweep is a storage utility and is not directed to children. It may be distributed internationally. Because no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no international transfer of your data by us.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this policy before releasing a version that materially changes these practices. For questions, use the support route below. Do not include passwords, Apple Account credentials, or payment information in a support request.
