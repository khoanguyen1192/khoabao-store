Support

Questions, problems, or a group Sweep got wrong — write to khoanguyen1192@gmail.com.

My storage did not go down

That is expected, and it is iOS rather than Sweep. Confirmed photos are moved to Photos → Recently Deleted, where they stay for 30 days. The space comes back when that album is emptied — open it in Photos and empty it if you want the space immediately.

Sweep says some photos were not compared

Those items live only in iCloud and their originals are not on your device. Sweep will not download them, because pulling gigabytes over your connection to check for duplicates is not a reasonable thing for an app to do without asking. To include them, open Photos and let the library download, or turn off Optimise iPhone Storage in Settings → Photos.

Sweep can only see a few photos

You chose Limited Access when granting permission, so iOS only hands Sweep the photos you picked. Change it in Settings → Apps → Sweep → Photos → Full Access, or pick more photos from inside the App.

It suggested keeping the wrong photo

Tap Choose myself in any group and pick the keeper. Sweep never discards a photo you have marked as a favourite or edited. If a group is grouped wrongly, tap Keep all — and please send an example, because that is the part worth getting right.

Restoring a purchase

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not to a Sweep account, because Sweep has no accounts.
