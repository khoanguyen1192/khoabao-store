Sweep suggests which photos are duplicates. You decide. Nothing is removed unless you confirm it, and iOS keeps removed items in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

1. What Sweep does

Sweep compares the photos and videos in your library on your device and groups the ones that appear to be the same shot. It suggests which one to keep and explains why. It also lists screenshots and unusually large videos.

2. What Sweep cannot do

Sweep can only work with your photo and video library. It cannot clear app caches, system files, or "other" storage — iOS does not permit any third-party app to do so, regardless of what similar apps claim.

Sweep does not permanently erase anything. Confirmed items are moved to Recently Deleted, and the space they occupy is reclaimed by iOS only once that album is emptied.

3. Your responsibility for what is removed

Grouping is automatic and can be wrong. Review each group before confirming. Sweep protects photos you have marked as favourites or edited, but you remain responsible for the deletions you approve. Keep an independent backup of anything irreplaceable.
