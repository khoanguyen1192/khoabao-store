PlatePact: 3D Print Planner helps small custom 3D-print shops compare a new request against existing commitments, visible work assumptions, and a conservative rework buffer—without connecting to printers or storing shop operations in the cloud.

Check the fit

Enter plates, slicer duration, a requested date, and the assumptions that matter. See on-track, conditional, or do-not-promise guidance.

Keep it honest

Every outcome identifies assumptions, schedule slack, and affected commitments. It is an estimate, not a delivery or profit guarantee.

Keep it local

Commitments, request labels, dates, prices, and files remain on your device. Optional analytics never include that content.

G-code assistance is experimental. Manual entry is always available and authoritative.
