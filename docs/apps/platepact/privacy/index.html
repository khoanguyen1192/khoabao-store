Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 31, 2026

PlatePact does not require an account. Your shop setup, commitment labels, request details, prices, dates, durations, and imported-file content are stored on your device and are not sent to us.

1. What stays on your device

PlatePact stores the shop assumptions, commitments, scenario inputs, and app preferences needed for local planning in app-specific device storage. The App does not provide an operational cloud account, customer portal, printer connection, marketplace connection, or data-sync service in this version.

You can erase this local information from PlatePact’s Shop settings or by deleting the App. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete data that remains only on your device.

2. Optional Firebase Analytics

By default, PlatePact may send limited product-interaction analytics through Google Firebase Analytics to help us understand whether the App works. You can turn this collection off at any time in Shop settings; the setting takes effect immediately for custom analytics events.

Our custom event allowlist uses only coarse categories such as onboarding step, manual-versus-G-code input mode, decision state, and coarse elapsed-time or commitment-impact buckets. We do not send customer names, request labels or notes, file names or paths, G-code/3MF/model content, material names, printer names or identifiers, exact prices, costs, margins, dates, deadlines, durations, or free-form text.

Firebase may process device, app, and usage identifiers and diagnostics as described in Google’s Firebase privacy information. We do not use Firebase for advertising, behavioral targeting, Firestore, Authentication, Storage, Functions, Crashlytics, or Remote Config in this version.

3. Purchases and RevenueCat

PlatePact may offer optional Pro products through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payment. RevenueCat processes anonymous app-user, purchase, entitlement, and transaction/receipt-related information to provide purchase validation, restoration, and entitlement access. We do not attach a RevenueCat identifier to a customer name, email address, shop data, request content, or operational records.

RevenueCat’s handling is described in its Privacy Policy. Purchase history and transaction information are used for app functionality, not advertising tracking.

4. File assistance

If you choose a local G-code file, PlatePact reads it on-device only to offer estimated duration or material metadata for your confirmation. The App does not upload or retain the model file by default. Unsupported, ambiguous, or unreadable files fall back to manual entry.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We do not run an operational backend or collect shop records. Google and RevenueCat retain information they process according to their own policies and legal obligations. Analytics can be disabled in the App; purchase records are controlled by Apple and RevenueCat according to their services.

6. Children and international users

PlatePact is a business-planning utility and is not directed to children. The App may be distributed internationally. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may process the limited information described above using global infrastructure under their applicable terms and safeguards.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this policy before releasing an App version that materially changes its data practices. For privacy questions, use the support route below. Do not include passwords, Apple ID credentials, payment information, customer details, or model files in a support request.
