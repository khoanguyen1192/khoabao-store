Privacy Policy

Updated November 7, 2026 · CarMuse 1.0 · KB Technologies

Your designs stay on your device

CarMuse stores widget designs, selected photos, messages, imported timed lyrics, streaming-source URLs and preferences on your device. The active design is shared with CarMuse's widget extension through the same App Group. Selected photos are accessed through Apple's photo picker. CarMuse does not upload these files to a CarMuse server or operate a user-account service.

Features you choose to use

Apple Music permission lets CarMuse read the selected track and playback position. Microphone access is requested when you choose song recognition, which uses Apple's recognition services. Streaming and AirPlay connect to the source or destination you choose; those operators may receive network and playback requests, including your IP address. Apple processes subscriptions and payments. CarMuse does not receive payment-card details. Optional drive reminders are scheduled locally.

Optional local weather

When you enable temperature on a widget, CarMuse asks for location permission while the app is in use. Approximate location is enough. The location is sent to Apple Weather to fetch current conditions; CarMuse does not save coordinates or build location history. Only the latest temperature, condition symbol, observation time and required attribution are shared locally with the widget. Refresh occurs when you open CarMuse or choose Refresh Weather. Readings older than one hour are hidden. Disable the temperature option or location permission to stop new lookups. Apple processes requests under its privacy policy.

Optional analytics and configuration

Usage analytics are off by default. If you enable them in Settings, Google Firebase Analytics processes app interactions, including a content-free widget-saved event, and technical information under Google's policies. Your photo, widget message, lyrics and playlist contents are not included in custom analytics events. Turning analytics off stops future analytics collection; it does not recall previously processed data. Firebase Remote Config requests configuration and processes country/language codes, time zone, OS version, app identifiers and technical installation/network information even when analytics is off.

CarMuse does not use an advertising SDK or request the advertising identifier. We do not sell your personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. See Firebase privacy information and Apple's privacy policy. Providers may process information outside your country and retain it under their published policies.

Your choices and retention

Delete saved widgets in My Widgets, remove imported content, turn off analytics or reminders in Settings, and change media permissions in iPhone Settings. Uninstalling removes local app data subject to Apple's backup and shared-container behavior. This does not cancel a subscription or delete Apple's transaction records. Manage subscriptions in your App Store account settings. For privacy questions or requests, contact kb.technologies.support@gmail.com; do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive media.

Changes

CarMuse is a general-audience personalization app. We update this policy when its data practices change. The current policy will be posted here.

Tiếng Việt

Thiết kế, ảnh đã chọn, lời nhắn, tệp lời bài hát và nguồn phát được lưu trên thiết bị. Widget đang dùng được chia sẻ với tiện ích của chính CarMuse. Phân tích sử dụng mặc định tắt; bạn có thể bật hoặc tắt trong Cài đặt. Firebase Remote Config vẫn có thể xử lý thông tin kỹ thuật để tải cấu hình. Quyền Apple Music và micro chỉ phục vụ thao tác bạn chọn. Khi bật nhiệt độ, vị trí gần đúng được gửi tới Apple Weather; CarMuse không lưu tọa độ hay lịch sử vị trí. Chỉ số đo mới nhất được lưu cục bộ và ẩn sau một giờ. Bạn có thể tắt nhiệt độ hoặc quyền vị trí. Apple xử lý thanh toán; nguồn phát/AirPlay bạn chọn xử lý kết nối tương ứng. Bạn có thể xóa widget và nội dung đã nhập, đổi quyền hoặc gỡ app. Gỡ app không hủy gói đăng ký. Liên hệ email hỗ trợ để hỏi về quyền riêng tư.
