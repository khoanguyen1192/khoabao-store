Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 8, 2026

FinishDial does not require an account and does not collect or transmit your coating, equipment, test-card, or recipe data. That information stays in app-specific storage on your device.

1. On-device information

FinishDial stores setup facts, safety confirmations, physical observations, controlled-test history, proven recipes, purchase-access state, and app preferences. The App has no operational cloud account, sync, remote recommendation engine, advertising, or analytics SDK in version 1.0.

You can remove local records inside the App where offered or by deleting the App. We cannot retrieve, restore, or remotely delete information that never leaves your device.

2. Purchases

Apple processes optional in-app purchases and restoration through your App Store account. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process purchase and device information under Apple's Privacy Policy. FinishDial checks Apple's signed transaction state on-device to unlock the lifetime entitlement.

3. Permissions and third parties

Version 1.0 does not request camera, photo library, microphone, contacts, location, notification, or tracking permission. It contains no third-party advertising, analytics, authentication, database, or crash-reporting SDK.

4. Support

If you choose to open a public GitHub support issue, GitHub processes the information you submit. Do not include customer names, job addresses, proprietary formulas, TDS/SDS files, photos, Apple ID details, credentials, or payment information.

5. Children, retention, and changes

FinishDial is a professional workflow utility and is not directed to children. On-device information remains until you remove it or delete the App; Apple retains transactions under its policies. We will update this page before releasing materially different data practices.

6. Contact

Use FinishDial Support for privacy questions. Developer: Khoa Nguyen. Bundle identifier: com.khoabao.finishdial.
