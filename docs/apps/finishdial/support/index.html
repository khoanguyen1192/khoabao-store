Support

Protect the work first.

FinishDial organizes physical test evidence. For product compatibility, exposure, fire, equipment, or coating-system questions, stop and use the exact manufacturer documents or contact the supplier.

Quick help

The app tells me to get the TDS/SDS

Use current documents for every component in the exact coating system. FinishDial deliberately blocks the loop when authoritative limits are missing.

I received a stop result

Preserve the physical cards and recorded setup. Do not continue on the production piece. Contact the coating or equipment supplier with the exact facts and completed tests.

How is a recipe marked proven?

After a paid guided test, record the one variable changed and the physical result. Only you can choose Improved and save that setup as a proven recipe.

How do I restore Lifetime?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used to buy FinishDial. If Apple reports no eligible purchase, check the account and connection before contacting support.

Where is my data?

Version 1.0 stores sessions and recipes locally on your device. Deleting the App can remove them; there is no cloud restore.

Contact

Open a FinishDial support request. GitHub issues are public: do not include customer names, job addresses, proprietary formulas, safety documents, photos, Apple ID details, credentials, or payment information.
