# QR Scanner Support

For help with QR Scanner (QR Code Scanner: Scan Barcodes), contact
[support@khoabao.store](mailto:support@khoabao.store).

Please include your app version, iOS version, device model, the steps that led
to the issue, and what you expected to happen. Do not send passwords, private
QR codes, payment details, or other sensitive data.

## Scanning

Allow camera access in iOS Settings to scan live codes. You can also choose a
photo or enter a code manually. Make sure the complete code is visible and the
image is in focus.

## Language

Open Settings → Language in the app and choose your preferred language.

## Subscriptions and restoring purchases

Use Restore in the app's subscription screen while signed in to the Apple
Account used for the original purchase. You can manage or cancel subscriptions
in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If an active purchase is not
recognized, contact support with the app version and the error message.

## Backups and inventory

Export a backup before replacing data or changing devices. Restoring a backup
replaces the app's current codes, projects and inventory transactions. CSV
export is available for reviewing inventory in spreadsheet tools.
